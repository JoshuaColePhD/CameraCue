This checklist preserves the full scope; an unchecked item is unfinished.

AMI pilot recordings and transcripts are CC BY 4.0. The pilot uses speaker IDs from the corpus meeting metadata, with whole speakers assigned to train, validation or held-out sets. AMI transcripts were reviewed by human transcribers; its word timestamps were generated by forced alignment. They are explicitly marked `forcedAlignment`, **not** manually checked boundaries. The pilot is conversational meeting speech from one room; it cannot establish general performance across accents, microphones or presentation settings. Source: https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/transcription.shtml and https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/download/.

Audio, user recordings and raw traces stay outside the Git repository. Offline mode measures recognition accuracy/throughput; it does not claim live latency. Real-time mode feeds PCM at the original 1x pace. No microphone or camera is activated by the file evaluator.
