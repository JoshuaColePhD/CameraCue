# CameraCue background images

Three original AI-generated stand-in backgrounds are bundled in `Sources/TeleprompterApp/Resources/`:

- `warm-studio.png`: warm plaster wall, oak shelves, plants, lavender/peach accents.
- `quiet-office.png`: ivory wall, bookshelf, sage and sky accents, daylight.
- `soft-landscape.png`: green hills and a pastel sunrise.

All are 1672 × 941 pixels. They are aspect-filled without stretching in the camera frame. They were created using the built-in imagegen tool, not downloaded stock photographs. Custom PNG/JPEG/HEIC/TIFF imports are normalized to a PNG up to 2560 pixels and copied into Application Support/CameraCue/Backgrounds so removing the original does not break the selected background.

## Final generation prompts

### Warm studio

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: built-in webcam virtual background for CameraCue. Create a wide 16:9, high-resolution 1920x1080 or larger image of an empty calm warm-white creative studio, subtle plaster wall, light oak shelf on one edge, small leafy plant, soft daylight. Camera at seated face height looking straight at the wall, broad uncluttered central area for a person to appear in front. Restrained lavender and peach accents, natural believable materials. No people, text, logos, watermarks, computer screens, desk in foreground. One full-bleed image.

### Quiet office

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: built-in webcam virtual background. Wide 16:9 high-resolution image of an empty tasteful home office background viewed from seated eye height. Warm ivory wall, softly out-of-focus bookshelf toward left edge, soft sky-blue and sage accents, sunlit window at far right edge. Spacious calm center for overlaying a speaker. Professional, natural, believable. No people, text, logos, watermarks or foreground desk. One full-bleed image.

### Soft landscape

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: built-in webcam virtual background. Wide 16:9 high-resolution peaceful landscape, distant softly rolling green hills with quiet blue sky and a little atmospheric haze, subtle pastel sunrise peach light. Low horizon, uncluttered composition, no prominent objects where a centered speaker will be composited. Natural photographic look, no people, buildings, text, logos or watermark. One full-bleed image.
